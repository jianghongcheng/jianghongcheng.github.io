Research

For GeoVIRA, I developed and trained geometry-conditioned models over a frozen medical image encoder, comparing cross-attention with measurement-aligned feature sampling for radiographic angle estimation. Evaluation uses five-fold out-of-fold landmark predictions, three paired detector/model seeds, and matched-versus-shuffled controls to test case-specific visual contributions. The foot dataset uses patient-level splits; the spine dataset uses image-level splits because patient identifiers are unavailable. Conditioning frozen visual features on geometry priors reduced geometry-only MAE by 13.5–25.1% across hallux valgus, intermetatarsal, and spinal Cobb angle tasks. Earlier research covers image restoration, hyperspectral pansharpening, NIR-to-RGB translation, and clinical NLP.

Selected Projects

SQL-Agent — SQL Data Agent
A Qwen3-14B natural-language SQL agent with browser and FastAPI/MCP interfaces for revenue totals, order lists, grouped summaries, and result review on registered SQLite data. Schema-aware generate–execute–repair loops enforce read-only authorization and query/output limits, with up to three attempts on a consistent data snapshot. General answers require human review.

Durable asynchronous execution includes idempotent jobs, renewable worker leases, retries, and stale-worker write protection, tested through worker termination, restart, duplicate-request, and timeout scenarios.

Configuration evaluation: 142/156 correct runs (91.0%), versus 102/156 for the single-pass Coder baseline, on 26 known development questions × 2 database instances × 3 trials. The selected configuration's p95 latency was 74.8 seconds, motivating asynchronous delivery. This is a configuration comparison—not a single-factor ablation, unseen benchmark, or production accuracy claim.

RadMeasure — Medical Imaging Measurement Agent
Constrains LLM-proposed actions to registered medical protocols and tools. A KEEP/REPAIR/STOP controller combines geometry verification, repair budgets, independent-proposal checks, human review, and execution traces. Supported foot measurements include hallux valgus and intermetatarsal angles.

Historical selective-repair evaluation: Across 528 runs on 176 archived cases, the policy intervened in 106 runs (20.1%), reducing mean angular error by 0.69° among intervened runs; overall MAE fell from 2.678° to 2.540°. These are archived repair-policy results, not current end-to-end clinical accuracy or 528 independent patients.

Research prototype. Not a medical device, and not approved for diagnosis or patient care.

Research

For GeoVIRA, I developed and trained geometry-conditioned models over a frozen medical image encoder, comparing cross-attention with measurement-aligned feature sampling for radiographic angle estimation. Evaluation uses five-fold out-of-fold landmark predictions, three paired detector/model seeds, and matched-versus-shuffled controls to test case-specific visual contributions. The foot dataset uses patient-level splits; the spine dataset uses image-level splits because patient identifiers are unavailable. Earlier research covers image restoration, hyperspectral pansharpening, NIR-to-RGB translation, and clinical NLP.
